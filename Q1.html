Tim Berners Lee: Inventor Of The World Wide Web

Sir Timothy (Tim) John Berners-Lee, born on June 8th, 1955, in London, United Kingdom, is an English computer scientist known for inventing the World Wide Web (1989), HTML (1989 and 1991), HTTP (1989 and 1990), and URL (1992). He is currently a professorial research fellow at the University of Oxford and a professor emeritus at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. Berners-Lee wrote his first proposal to CERN—the European Organization for Nuclear Research in Switzerland—proposing a “universally linked information system” to help physicists share information across different computers, and the World Wide Web later made its public debut on August 6th, 1991.
